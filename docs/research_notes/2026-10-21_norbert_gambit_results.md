# Norbert's gambit vs the broker's FX conversion

Cost parameters (commission, spreads, FX tiers): see [`docs/cost_assumptions.md`](../cost_assumptions.md).

Data fetched 2026-10-21 (yfinance Ticker.history (auto_adjust=False); BoC Valet FXUSDCAD). Generated by `scripts/cost_edge_norbert.py`; the spread, deviation, volatility and saving figures are computed from the local snapshot in `research/cost_edge_norbert/`. The broker's tiers, commission and tick size are input constants read from the broker's published fee pages on 2026-10-18 and were not re-verified here.

## 1. Data coverage (read first)

- **DLR-U.TO daily history on Yahoo is essentially absent**: the daily endpoint returned one row on 2026-10-21, and the hourly endpoint starts on 2026-07-17 (66 sessions to 2026-10-21). Daily bars for DLR-U.TO are therefore built from the 60-minute bars (open of first bar, max high, min low, close of the last bar).
- So the implied-rate deviation and the DLR-U.TO spread exist **only for 2026-07-17 to 2026-10-21**. No older period is stitched in or proxied. Treat every NG number below as a ~3-month sample.
- DLR.TO has a long daily history, used only for its per-year spread estimates and for its volatility.

## 2. Implied rate vs Bank of Canada

Implied rate = DLR.TO close / DLR-U.TO close, both from the last 60-minute bar of the session (same source, same bar). The BoC daily rate FXUSDCAD is a truncated mean of 480 one-minute indicative mid quotes between 08:00 and 16:00 ET (top and bottom 2.5% dropped), published by 16:30 ET ([method](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/background-information-on-foreign-exchange-rates/)). It is a session average, not a closing rate, so the deviation against ~16:00 closes is mostly averaging-vs-close noise, not cost. **The base case therefore sets d = 0 and does not credit the mean deviation**; the p95 adverse deviation is kept as a separate sensitivity. 66 sessions in section 1, 65 with a BoC rate: 2026-09-30 has no rate (National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, the Bank of Canada was closed). Positive deviation = implied rate above BoC (CAD->USD loses, USD->CAD gains).

## 3. Half-spread estimates (ESTIMATES, no quote data)

Half-spread as % of price. CS = Corwin-Schultz (2012) on two-day windows; AR = Abdi-Ranaldo (2017), one value per calendar month with at least 8 observations. Negative per-window estimates are clamped to 0 (standard convention). Half-tick = $0.005 / median close (the physical minimum: both lines trade in $0.01 ticks). **Used = max(CS, AR, half-tick)** - the half-tick term goes one step beyond the brief because on tick-bound ETFs the high-low estimators can sit below the minimum tick.

## 4. Cost model

c_FX(V) = listed_spread(tier) x 4/3 (+ m_aff, set to 0). c_NG(V) = 2k/V + h_DLR + h_DLRU + d, k = C$9.99, d = 0 in the base case. V is the CAD-equivalent notional; USD-equivalent uses the latest BoC rate 1.4057 (2026-10-21). The broker's tier is read off the *Gross Transaction Amount*, whose currency basis is UNVERIFIED, so both bases are shown: **USD basis** (amount = V / BoC, primary, matches the page's USD examples) and **CAD basis**.

## 5. Journal-delay risk (risk, NOT a cost)

After the journal you sell the *other* line, so the risk is the price risk of the line you sell: CAD->USD sells DLR-U.TO (USD-quoted, close to cash; the CAD->USD rate was locked when you bought DLR.TO, so what remains is tick noise), USD->CAD sells DLR.TO (effectively USD/CAD volatility). 1-sigma = daily log-return std x sqrt(days); std: DLR-U.TO 0.114% (overlap window, mostly tick bounce), DLR.TO 0.453% (full history), 0.299% (overlap window).

| delay (business days) | CAD->USD 1-sigma bp | USD->CAD 1-sigma bp (full history) | C$ at 100k, CAD->USD | C$ at 100k, USD->CAD | C$ at 500k, USD->CAD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.0 | 0.0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 1 | 11.4 | 45.3 | 114 | 453 | 2,267 |
| 3 | 19.7 | 78.5 | 197 | 785 | 3,926 |

Whether the second line can be sold before the journal settles (which would remove the delay) is UNVERIFIED.

## 7. Caveats, verified and UNVERIFIED items

**Input constants, not re-verified:** the broker's tiers, commission and tick size are taken from the broker's published fee pages as read on 2026-10-18 and were not re-checked against the broker's pages by this script. Currency Conversion Spread tiers (cash FX): 1.26% below $25,000; 0.78% for $25,000-$99,999; 0.46% for $100,000-$499,999; 0.27% for $500,000-$999,999; 0.19% from $1,000,000; the one-third affiliate surcharge (all-in = listed x 4/3); the in-trade sentence ('$60,000 or more' is charged the $50,000-$59,999 tier); the $9.99 commission and the tick size $0.01.

**UNVERIFIED** (each one is a stated assumption above):

- Whether DLR / DLR.U is on the broker's $0 'Select ETFs' list: the pricing page names no tickers (a link to the list was not followed), so $9.99 per trade is kept.
- Whether the DLR.U sale is charged in USD (the variant column in 4.3a and 4.3b shows the effect).
- The currency basis of the broker's 'Gross Transaction Amount' (both bases shown; notionals near a tier boundary give different savings).
- m_aff, the margin of the broker's affiliate rate over mid-market, is unknown and set to 0: The FX desk's true cost is higher, NG savings are lower bounds.
- the desk's journaling procedure and how many business days it takes (0, 1, 3 modelled as risk only).
- Whether the second line can be sold before the journal settles (would remove the delay risk).
- The Global X fund terms (issuer pages returned HTTP 403 earlier).
- Half-spreads are ESTIMATES (Corwin-Schultz, Abdi-Ranaldo, half-tick floor), not quotes. Depth and market impact are not captured, which matters most at C$250k-500k; check live bid/ask and size before a large trade.
- The deviation sample is one quarter of sessions (n in section 2); the BoC rate is a session mean, so the deviation vs ~16:00 closes is mostly averaging noise (the base case does not credit it), and the adverse (p95) deviation partly overlaps the half-spread (closes sit inside the quotes), so adding both is conservative.
