## 3. Price-only bound on short-put assignment

Universe: top 40 XIC weights at 2026-02-17 (panel, read-only); 40 of 40 had usable Yahoo history; 2005-03-30..2026-09-23. **SURVIVOR-ONLY**: current constituents with full histories, so drift is overstated and left-tail frequencies are understated; treat as a lower bound on assignment.
Event = day with drawdown from the 20-day high in [-10%, -5%], de-clustered to one event per 21 trading days per ticker. Forward return = 21-day adjusted close return. Strike: Black-Scholes 10%-delta put with sigma = trailing 60-day realised vol (r=q=0), so X varies by row; 'hit' = forward return < -X (put finishes ITM = assignment).

| sample | n | mean fwd | median fwd | 5th pct | mean X | P(ret < -X) | P(ret < -5%) | P(ret < -10%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| unconditional (all days) | 201,270 | 1.41% | 1.22% | -11.53% | 9.18% | 8.22% | 17.16% | 6.62% |
| after 5-10% drawdown | 4,872 | 1.72% | 1.43% | -12.83% | 10.74% | 7.10% | 20.57% | 8.42% |

Strikes use realised vol; market IV is usually above RV, which puts a true 10-delta strike further OTM and lowers the hit rate shown. Events are cross-sectionally clustered in crash periods (2008, 2020), so the conditional-vs-unconditional gap is descriptive, not a significance claim.

## 4. Verdict (rule-based on the numbers above)

- Index-level premium after 2012: BXM-minus-SPX CAGR -715 bp/yr, CAPM alpha -74 bp/yr (t -0.60).
- PUT (put-write) post-2012 CAPM alpha 31 bp/yr (t 0.27).
- Retail covered-call cost at the three sizes: 143, 107, 99 bp/yr (open only).
- After retail costs the index premium does not cover costs at any tested size; buying MX/ORATS data is NOT justified on the covered-call premium alone.
- Short-put side: after a 5-10% drawdown P(assignment-type loss) = 7.10% vs 8.22% unconditional (survivor-only lower bound).

## Limits

- Cboe indices are SPX, not XIU/TSX; no TSX option data used. Index returns are gross of any retail cost and tax.
- Half-spread and commission mix are assumptions; the real XIU spread is exactly what is unmeasured.
- Survivor-only equity panel; realised-vol strikes instead of market IV.
